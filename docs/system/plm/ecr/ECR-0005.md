# LDO 300 mA rating vs ~315 mA bridge peaks

Status: proposed
Raised: 2026-10-01 by claude
Items changed: SUB-POWER, SUB-OUTPUT

## Change

The TPS7A2030 LDO is rated 300 mA (current limit 360 mA min) but the bridge pulls ~315 mA peaks at full
drive into 8 ohm (3.0 V / (8 + 1.2 ohm FETs + 0.1 shunt)). Average at listening level is only 0.6-1.7 mA and
C14 22 uF buffers peaks, but full-scale self-test tones or a 12 vs 8 ohm exciter change the picture.
Options to evaluate in the simplification study: firmware drive cap; larger LDO (e.g. RT9080 600 mA);
an output stage powered from VBAT/VSYS directly.
