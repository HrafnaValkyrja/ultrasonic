## Change

Source: docs/research/simplification-study.md (2026-10-02). **RECOMMENDED package.** Final-size integration: A plus the bridge and self-test cuts, the CC cut, the LED on the lid and the mechanical stack that makes rev 1 final-size (O20). Schematic changes are batched into the next revision; gen.py and the shell files are untouched by this ECR.

**Members**: everything in ECR-0014 (Package A), plus
- OUT-01 delete R4, R6 (N-gate pull-downs); R3/R5 P-gate pull-ups stay. Firmware rule: N pins idle-low before any P pin leaves analog (OSSI = 1, MOE after). Narrows D6 "pulls mandatory" to the P side (owner confirms).
- OUT-03 F4 by synchronous R21 sampling (TIM1 TRGO2 + OC5/OC6, shortest sample time, DMA raw samples): delete C22 and the offset resistor; signed coil current with phase. Requires OUT-07.
- OUT-05 C14 22 uF 0603 (C59461) -> 1 uF 0402 (C52923, existing line). +4 mV carrier ripple at the ceiling: scope TP4. Requires OUT-07.
- PER-01D (gated by a keying test that needs a $5.21 sample pair, which O21 blocks; OFF by default unless O21 is lifted) delete R18 and J12; 4-contact Xinyangze dock (target C42463273, 19 in stock; head C5126845, 45 in stock; Rd 5.1k in the cable's USB-C plug, or a USB-A plug). With 4 contacts at 2.5 mm pitch a 180-degree mate swaps 1<->4 and 2<->3, so no pin order is rotation-safe: **pass = the rotated head repels on the exact pair to be bought; any partial hold fails; fail = the 5-contact target with VBUS on the centre pin and R18/J12 kept (50 placements instead of 49)**. A USB-C receptacle fallback would need 2 x 5.1k Rd on CC1/CC2 at the receptacle wiring. D4 stays either way. Record the contact order in gen.py and sub-dock-usb when the sample is in hand.
- PER-07 (gated by the O8 reversal and E1; E1 needs exciters, which O21 blocks; OFF by default unless O21 is lifted) power LED onto the pod board F face behind a lid light pipe (D0.9-1.0 bore, clear epoxy, boss): arm 4 -> 2 wires, pad board deleted, J7/J8 gone. The heel channel, strut bore and counterbore **stay** Ø1.0 / Ø1.2 / Ø1.6 (a 2-wire bundle only adds slack; the heel has 196 degrees of R0.8 bends that cannot be reamed). LED PWM exactly 200.0225 kHz (1 x fs_out) or 400.045 kHz (LN-R11/LF-6); the O8 reversal covers the lid site AND non-solid status patterns; patterns run in Run mode (TIM4 stops in Stop 2: sub-ui issue 6).
- PER-12 PB6 USART1_TX hook and 4 via-dot escapes (PB6; PB8 and PB1 for the MDF mic-clock fallback of sub-audio-in issue 5; the MIC_DATA net, with R2's mic-side pad as the clock end). Alternative: state the fallback is dropped (owner decision; hardware-only risk) and let C use PB1 for /PG.
- Mechanical: SIZ-01 board 34 x 13 -> 28 x 12 (freeze only after the wear dummies); SIZ-02 board hung from the lid (2 printed 0.7 mm pins, 2 NPTH 0.9 mm, 4 VHB 0.25 mm spots; ribs, foam, clamp bands out) **with an SW1 back-stop** (a 1.4 mm compressed silicone or PE foam dot on the cell top directly under SW1, or a printed post stopping the board 0.1-0.15 mm early; R21 and every other B part out of that footprint), because the 1.2-2.0 N press (600k cycles) is otherwise reacted by VHB in tension and can eat the KMT0's 0.15 +/- 0.1 mm stroke (sub-ui issue 2); SIZ-03 F gap 1.25 mm; SIZ-04 lid wall 0.8 mm (plate kept); SIZ-06 dock tails bent flat (belly 3.5 -> 2.05 mm, after a spare-target bend and pull test; **it spends O16(3)**: the USB-C receptacle, 2.76 mm tall with a 2.56 mm mouth, no longer fits, so the fallback survives only as a reprinted shell with the belly back at 3.5 mm, +391 mm3 on the B core); SIZ-14 wire stowage; ASM-09 pad order (shared GND pad between J3 and J5; every post-bond hand pad on the B face).

**Totals (computed)**: placed parts 56 -> 49 (R11 R17 C20 R19 R18 R4 R6 C22 out, LED in); BOM lines per order 30 -> 27; Extended types per order 11 -> 12 (R21); BOM $/pod +0.12; $/order -14.10 (parts +0.49, BOM-line fees -4.59, pad board not ordered about -10, derived and unquoted); courtyards 246 -> 214 mm2 on a 28 x 12 board (31.9 % density; the proven routed density was 42.9 %); pod 7.80 -> 6.83 cm3 (-12.5 %), height with belly 18.7 -> 16.55 mm, 13.60 -> 13.15 mm off the temple, mass 12.42 -> 11.96 g; hand wires 12 -> 9 (pads 12 -> 8), arm wires 4 -> 2; MCU pins listed free 9 (8 unassigned because PB6 carries the TX hook; 4 clean: PH0, PH1, PA3, PA10); +3V0 peak about 92 mA; average current -0.03 mA with the FW-1 over-current guard off (on: +0.15 to +0.34 mA). **B3 (PER-01D and PER-07 off, no sample needed): 49 placements, 28 BOM lines, 12 wires / 11 pads, 4 arm wires, the same 6.83 cm3.**
