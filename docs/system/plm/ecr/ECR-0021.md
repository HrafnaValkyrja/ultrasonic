## Change

Owner default for queue A-K4-DOCK (commit 763c337). Evidence: `docs/research/k4-dock.yaml` option_A_design; `hw/mech/dims_k4.py`, `hw/mech/shell_k4.py`; `sim/checks/dock_pull.py`.

Replaces the Xinyangze YZT0675 5-pin magnetic target with the pod's own contacts, mating the YZP0048-20048-04025-03 4-pin pogo head (LCSC C5126845, stock 46 on 2026-10-08):
- 4 castellated half-hole ENIG pads on an 0.8 mm tab of board M (pitch 2.5; net order J3 DOCK_VBUS, J11 USB_DM, J10 USB_DP, J4 GND), 4 belly windows 1.3 x 1.2 in the 0.6 mm belly wall (1.2 webs).
- 2 N52 D2.5 x 1.0 magnets in bossed pockets (pod x 19.15 and 32.35, y 6.8), opposite-pole keying against the head.
- Pod grows 1.5 mm at the front (L 49.15 -> 50.65; T 6.4, H 14.8 unchanged). M mid-plane pinned at y 6.8, M parts on its floor face only.
- D4/D5/U6 are already on M (ECR-0020), so no new part. Builds on ECR-0019 (4 dock wires, no CC) and ECR-0020 (M carries the dock). The YZT0675 (C5126848) leaves the BOM.

## Evidence and limits

- Pull (sim/checks/dock_pull.py, idealised Coulomb model, real 0.7-0.9x): 2 x D2.5x1.0 about 2.2 N at 0.4 mm gap vs 1.18 N spring push-off (4 x 30 gf): net about +1.0 N; D2x0.5 / D3x0.5 that fit the wall alone are zero or negative.
- Fits (shell_k4.py 2026-10-08): wall 0.6 holds, tab-to-cell 0.55, boss top to cell 0.15 (tightest).
- [T] unverified: head magnet pitch, polarity, stroke, tip diameter (caliper a C5126845 sample first; O21 holds the order); sweat sealing of 4 windows; magnet sourcing; JLC castellation on a roughly 11 x 12 mm board.
- Fallback A2: bought 4-pad dock chip glued in a belly window.
