## Change

Source: docs/research/simplification-study.md (2026-10-02). Package C = Package B (ECR-0015) **plus** PWR-01: U3 BQ25180 (C3682423, TI 1 A I2C linear charger with power path, 8-ball 0.4 mm DSBGA) -> BQ25186DLHR (C44639442, same family and I2C address 0x6A, leadless WSON-10 2.2 x 2.0 x 0.8 mm with exposed pad, adds /PG and /CE). Approving C implies approving B; C alone is only the U3 swap. This is a **robustness swap, not a simplification**: no part is removed and the footprint grows 3.2 mm2.

**Why you might take it**: hand-reworkable pads instead of a 1.6 x 0.9 mm DSBGA; exposed-pad heat path (RthetaJA 68.3 vs 107.1 C/W: die +20 K instead of +31 K at 0.29 W at the start of constant-current charge); a hardware /PG level that works with I2C dead; /CE as a hardware charge inhibit; removes the ball-escape routing problem (3 of the 7 unrouted nets in the draft). **Why you might not**: +3.2 mm2; still an X-ray-only package; pads are 0.2 mm wide (not a JLC-process win); stock 301-451 and falling; newer part (rev A, Jan 2025); the datasheet SLUSF69A contradicts itself in six places (VINDPM reset, pushbutton input in ship mode, Device_ID, ILIM 400 vs 380 mA, battery FET 115 vs 55 mohm, VIN range); BATFET 115 vs 55 mohm; input OVP 18.5 V instead of 5.7 V leaves D3 the only sub-6 V guard.

**Totals (computed)**: as B except BOM $/pod -0.14 (BQ25186 $1.7818 vs BQ25180 $2.0419 at 2026-10-02T05:22Z), $/order -15.14, courtyards 218 mm2, one hidden-joint 0.4 mm DSBGA replaced by a leadless WSON. Placed parts 49, BOM lines 27, Extended types 12, pod 6.83 cm3, pins listed free 9 (8 unassigned, 4 clean); the optional /PG on PB1 is excluded while the MDF mic fallback stays (taking it leaves 7 unassigned).

**Firmware deltas**: VINDPM has no 4.2 V option (VBAT+300 mV / 4.5 V / 4.7 V / off) and termination is disabled while VINDPM is active: plan VINDPM off or bench-measure VIN at the pin first; read back every reset value (VINDPM, Device_ID, EN_PUSH, PB_LPRESS_ACTION, ILIM, RON_BAT); optional SYS_REG battery tracking (cuts U4 docked heat 12-47 %), IBAT_OCP about 500 mA backstop (accuracy unspecified), ILIM 300 mA, charge voltage as a knob (VBATREG default 4.20 V; 4.10-4.15 V costs 1-2 h worst-case runtime); /CE tied low; watchdog rule (WATCHDOG_SEL = 11 before ROM DFU) unchanged.

**Gates**: owner approves O16(2) (it names BQ25180); a fresh audit of SLUSF69A (audit-datasheet-claims.md was done on SLUSE99C); new symbol and DLH0010A footprint (3 GND vias to In1 filled or beside the pad, 0.125 mm stencil per TI); part_heights.yaml row (0.8 mm); JLCDFM verdict; reserve spares at once. Failure classes: register differences firmware-fixable only if read back on board 1; footprint and exposed-pad soldering hardware-only.

**O21/O22**: as B (ECR-0015): C inherits B's gates and the O21 decision (study 3.9); a new datasheet audit (SLUSF69A) and board-1 read-back are rev-1 measurements under O22.

**Open ECRs**: as B; additionally ECR-0002 items 1-2 become moot (no ball escape), ECR-0013 F1 (VINDPM 4.2 V) and S3 (TI now asks 35 V caps on IN) are rewritten, ECR-0008 reserves BQ25186. Do not combine with PWR-11 (BQ25185 standalone, rejected).
