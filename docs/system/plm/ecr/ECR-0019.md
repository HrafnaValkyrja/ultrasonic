# Drop the pod USB-C fallback: Rd to the dock head, remove J12/R18/D6 (O31)

Raised: 2026-10-08 by claude

## Change

Owner ruling O31 (2026-10-08): drop the pod's sealed USB-C fallback if the magnetic dock works; the board may shrink accordingly.
Proposal: the USB-C-only parts leave the pod and Rd (5.1 kohm CC pull-down; a USB-C source only turns on VBUS when it sees Rd) moves into the cable-side dock head.
- Remove from the pod: J12 (CC wire pad, 1.0 mm), R18 (5k1 0201, C270344), D6 (TPD1E10B06DPYR, TI 1-channel 10 V ESD diode, X1SON-2, on CC at J12).
- Dock wires 5 -> 4 (J3 DOCK_VBUS, J4 GND, J10 USB_DP, J11 USB_DM); pod wires 12 -> 11. The Xinyangze YZT0675 target (5 contacts) stays; its 5th contact is left unwired.
- Rd moves to the head: 5k1 from the head's CC conductor to GND (a USB-A host cable needs no Rd).
- The USB-C receptacle reservation (O16(3), Same Sky UJ32-C-H-G-MSMT) is dropped: R-DOCK-BODY keep-out text and DK-10 close. Per O31 the fallback if the dock fails is a clip-on USB-C adapter outside the pod.
- Overtakes the CC-out half of ECR-0015 PER-01D/SIZ-06 without changing the 5-contact target (MZD-9 no longer binds).

NOT done here: gen.py (l.22, 99, 300, 308-309), system_map.py l.62, BOM, the board, dock_route.py: the board is being re-planned in the K4 thin-concept study; they change in the commit that adopts this ECR.

## Risks and checks

1. Dock head must carry Rd. A USB-C-to-C cable with Rd in the head makes the host switch VBUS on whenever the cable is plugged in, even undocked (5 V on exposed contacts; sweat shorts). Options: (a) USB-C host cable, Rd in head [recommended: works with chargers and bench ports; head contacts must be recessed/shrouded]; (b) USB-A host cable, no Rd (A ports source 5 V regardless: same exposure); (c) keep CC in the pod (status quo). Owner decides with the head design (DK-01/DK-02).
2. ROM DFU / USB FS unaffected? Expected yes: both use only PA11/PA12 (D-/D+) with VBUS sense on PA1 (R12/R13); CC is not sensed since Rev F, and UCPD dead-battery pull-downs are held off by the PB5 strap (ECR-0013 S1). Not yet verified in the datasheet/AN2606 text: re-read DFU entry conditions, then a first-article enumeration over the dock (bring-up step 6).
3. ILIM by enumeration: host default current unchanged; the charger limit is firmware-set (ECR-0009).
4. ESD on the remaining pads: D5 (TPD1E10B06) stays on DOCK_VBUS, U6 (TPD2E2U06) on D+/D-; D6 only covered CC, so no pod contact is left unprotected. CC protection, if wanted, lives in the head.
5. The 5-pin head (C5126847) has stock 0 (DK-01): a head or cable adapter with a 5k1 is a hand build; show it in the dock build sheet.
6. No in-pod USB-C if the magnets fail (wear untested, O16-connector stage D); O31 condition: prove the dock at bring-up.
