## Pad housing change (largest impact)
| | RC-BC02 (today) | TEAX14C02-8 |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | 6.0 x 4.0 x 12.6, slender, long axis along the arm; cup outer width 7.9 | dia 14 x 9.85 round |
| Cup cavity | 6.3 x 12.9 | about dia 14.3 round, 9.85+ deep |
| Mass | 1.2 g (estimate, Low) | **about 12.99 g (audiophonics listing; a shipping weight may include packaging)** |
- Pad grows from 7.9 mm to about 15.5 mm wide, and the vertical cup axis (RC-BC02 lies along the arm because upright it clashes with the Ear open hook, reg-pad point 4) becomes a 14 mm disc that must sit flat on the tragus. A disc of that size contradicts the 7.9 mm hex-collar / NiTi socket geometry and the Ø8 contact.
- **Mass is the blocker to size up**: +about 11.8 g on the NiTi arm tip vs a whole pod of 9.4-10.6 g worn (ECR-0024 numbers). Spring force at the tragus, COM and the "2 h wear" test (ECR-0006 dummy pair) all change. Needs a weighted dummy of 13 g before any print.
- Thickness 9.85 mm vs today's cap face 7.5 mm from skin: about +2.4 mm stack toward the head side; recheck the Ear-open clearance.
- Contact: the 14 mm face is the radiating surface; a 100-150 mm2 near-flat Sugru/silicone pad (O16(4)) fits inside the face; stiffness shapes the +4 dB pad-stiffness item in R-exciter2.

## Wiring, connector, current through the pad lead
- Still 2 exciter leads + 2 LED wires = the same 4 arm wires (J1/J2 OUT_A/OUT_B, J7/J8 LED). Terminal style of the TEAX14 (solder tabs vs flying leads) is unknown until the datasheet is read; the pad board's two plated holes for the leads (reg-pad) may need a different land.
- Current: unchanged. The OUT-07 clamp limits the drive current at **208 mA** (FWSIM-R64), so the arm wires and litz carry the same current; the load only changes voltage.
- Thermal/power rating: 0.8 W rated vs about 0.17 W at clamp: ample.

## Bridge load at 7.8 ohm vs today
Today modelled at 8 ohm (loudness.md): 208 mA -> terminals 1.66 V peak / 1.18 V rms, bridge differential 1.93 V, **173 mW**. At 7.8 ohm (DC resistance, current-driven so the clamp stays exact): 1.62 V peak, 1.15 V rms, **169 mW** (I^2 R / 2), i.e. -2.5 %. Bridge, R21 sense, U4 loading: effectively unchanged. Unverified: voice-coil inductance Le and the 4 kHz-and-up impedance rise (datasheet), which sets the headroom at 6 kHz; and whether the clamp holds the same 208 mA at the 200 kHz PWM with the larger L (check with ngspice R-L(-motional) load before apply). The gain (+7.6 dB) is therefore force, not extra current. Reference: FWSIM-R64 clamp 208 mA = 0.8 x cell pulse 260 mA.
