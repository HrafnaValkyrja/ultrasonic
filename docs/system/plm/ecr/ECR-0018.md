## Change

Phase 2 (owner O26: miniaturization now, internals first; O25: Claude lays out, owner reviews in 1-2 weeks). Package set
"MZ-2 Balanced" from docs/research/miniaturization-prelim.md, decided by Claude on the research + skeptic record
(docs/research/mini/skeptic.md); every choice is reversible and goes to the owner review.

Choices (MZD ids from the synthesis):
- MZD-1 face strategy: one-face (all parts on B, SW1 alone on F in a lid pocket), GATED by a placement trial at board
  length <= 32 mm x 12 mm incl. JLC spacing; fallback MZ-2b (two-face, F parts <= 0.65 mm).
- MZD-2 MCU: keep STM32U575CIU6Q QFN48 (rework/probing, O18/O19); WLCSP only if the trial fails by a margin.
- MZD-3 passives: vetted 0201 set (gen.py MZ2 table, 29 parts incl. Y1/D4/R21); R1/R20 hooks stay 0402; C8/C9 stay 0402
  10 V (SMPS loop, 0201 ESR at 3 MHz unproven); bulk/25 V parts unchanged.
- MZD-4 R21: 0402 Panasonic ERJ2BSFR10X current sense (OUT-02 was deferred to Phase 2), at the board edge for lifting.
- MZD-5 C14: keep 22 uF 0603 (B height is mic-pinned; OUT-07 clamp decision pending).
- MZD-6 layers: design to 4-layer 0.8 mm with POFV only where fan-out needs it; 6L if a live quote (owner login, not an
  order) shows 0.8 mm at an acceptable price.
- MZD-7 shell: SW1 lid pocket + the owner-approved sealed duct ID 1.0 with an x-stop (O24); bond method decided with the
  shell work.
- MZD-8 no dock FPC; MZD-9 no reversal of owner decisions (O8 LED in pad, 5-contact dock, R4/R6); MZD-10 wire pads on B,
  1.0 mm, with >= 0.8 mm copper gaps between pads whose bridge would be harmful (Phase-1 lesson: J4-J5 0.60 mm).
Implementation: gen.py POD_PACKAGES=mz2 -> pod_mz2.net / bom_jlc_mz2.csv (nets identical to Rev G, verified); the
default switches to mz2 when the Phase-2 board replaces the draft (checks, sch.py, bom.py, part_heights, bom_check
identity, sourcing lock updated in that commit).
