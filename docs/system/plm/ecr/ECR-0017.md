## Change

Owner decision O24 (Phase-1 briefing, 2026-10-02).
- C8/C9: ST DS13737 Rev 10 p.153 requires VDD11 COUT rated >= 10 V; the 2026-10-01 swap to 6.3 V C12530 is reverted to C107369 (Samsung CL05A225KP5NSNC 2.2 uF 10 V X5R 0402, same footprint). JLC Standard PCBA charges the feeder fee per BOM line, Basic or Extended, so the swap's cost reason is void.
- D6: the CC dock contact (J12) was the only exposed contact without ESD; D6 = TPD1E10B06 (same part and BOM line as D5, C48260 qty 2), on B behind J12 at (32.6, 6.2), one via.
