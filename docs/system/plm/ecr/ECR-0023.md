# K4 floor post on U1 top under the BM28 line: SW1 press path lid-M-BM28-P-U1-post-floor, shim-fitted gap (ECR-0022 follow-up)

Status: proposed
Raised: 2026-10-08 by claude
Items changed: REG-POD-BODY, SUB-UI, REG-BOARD, PHYSICAL

## Change

ECR-0022 left SW1 press failing on the 19 mm2 ledge bond (sw1_press_fem.py K4 ledge mode, 2 N: SW1 153 / 49 / 16 / 6 um at VHB E 0.15 / 0.5 / 2 / 10 MPa vs KMT022 50 um min; VHB tension peak 113-187 kPa vs 85).

Change: a printed floor post (2.4 x 2.4, tub floor, pod x of board 8.2, on the centre line) bearing on the U1 top (QFN48 body, P floor face) so a press goes lid -> M -> BM28 housing -> P -> U1 -> post -> floor, closing after a fitted gap of 0.015 mm (0.000-0.030 after fit). The ledge VHB becomes a hold-in, not the load path.

Why U1 and not a free spot, and why not under M: P's floor face is U1 under the whole BM28 line (J21 x 6.2-13.4, centre line). A post at a part-free spot (x < 4, or the y 9-11.5 row) is 3-7 mm off the plug line and loads the plug as a 2 N x 3-7 mm couple (P tips about the post, prying the 0.35 mm contacts that are rated 10 cycles). A post under M through a P cutout is the shortest path and bypasses the BM28, but P has C7/R21/C22/U1 exactly there: it needs a P re-place and re-route (not allowed in this change). The press direction compresses the BM28 housing stops (mating direction), the safe direction. U1 top load 0.35 MPa at 2 N, 1.7 MPa at 10 N.

Tolerance: floor-side chain (VHB 0.0375, M 0.1, P 0.1, BM28 0.05, floor print 0.1, lid print 0.1, U1 0.05) = +-0.537 linear, +-0.215 RSS. A fixed-height post cannot be effective and never negative at once, so it is fitted: with the stack bonded to the lid, measure U1 top depth vs post top depth, add PET/Kapton shim or trim the post to gap 0.015 (+-0.015). Printed at the nominal gap.

Results (fenced): SW1 at 2 N with the post, gap 0..30 um: 4.6-34 um (E 0.15), 3.9-31 um (E 0.5); VHB tension 7-23 kPa (E 0.15), 19-62 kPa (E 0.5). E 2: passes tension only for gap <= ~0.01 (80 kPa at 0.01, 120 kPa free): marginal [A]. Heights: floor worst +0.163, lid +0.112 unchanged; pod T unchanged. Cost: nothing on the boards; tub +5.9 mm3; assembly +1 measurement and shim step.
Integration map: REG-POD-BODY (post, fit), SUB-UI (press path), REG-BOARD (U1 top is a bearing surface; no board change), PHYSICAL (stack chain).


## Addendum 2026-10-08: compressible pad evaluated instead of the shim-fitted post (owner builds by hand: no trim/shim fits). RESULT: does not close, owner decision needed

Geometry correction: Y_F is 8.75 (F_GAP 0.95), so U1 top is only 1.05 above the tub floor (not 1.7). Floor-side chain stays +-0.5375 linear.
Model: sim/checks/sw1_pad_fem.py (new; reuses sw1_press_fem.py K4 ledge plate+VHB, pad = nonlinear unilateral spring under the BM28 line, 27 corners: pad thickness +-10 %, chain +-0.5375, CFD min/typ/max). Output sim/out/mech/sw1_pad_fem.json.
Inputs (Rogers PDS, fetched 2026-10-08): PORON 4701-30 Very Soft 20 lb (4701-30-20062/20093-04 class, PSA backed): CFD@25 % 21/35/55 kPa, comp. set 2 % @23 C, thickness 1.57-3.18 mm +-10 % (Pub 17-006, 0723-PDF); BISCO HT-800 silicone foam: CFD 67 kPa typ (41-97), 0.79-12.7 mm (Rogers PDS). The sheets give only the 25 % point; curve shape beyond is [A] (generic foam + densification). A gap pad was not modelled (same physics: <= 30 % recommended compression, tolerance +-10 %, needs more height than 1.05).
Results at 2 N press, E_vhb 0.5 MPa, 27 corners:
- 1.57 mm Poron: loses contact at the worst corner (d0 -0.175..+1.21), so a corner has no support (VHB tension 113 kPa).
- 2.36 mm Poron, 1.5 or 2.4 mm square: contact at every corner (d0 +0.54..+2.08, up to 80 % strain), but static preload up to 1.6 N (1.5 sq) / 3.6 N (2.4 sq), pushing SW1 toward the lid by 31 / 70 um, i.e. into the 70 um PRE_GAP, and VHB tension peak still 110-112 kPa (> 85), SW1 ~49 um (no margin). HT-800 is stiffer: preload 2.7-5.6 N, 51-107 um.
- Larger pads / 3.18 mm: preload 4-16 N, 150-370 um shift: worse.
Why it cannot close: the tolerance span (~1.6 mm with pad thickness) is wider than a foam's useful compression window, so a pad soft enough not to pre-close SW1 (<= 0.2-0.3 N) is too soft to back a 2 N press (needs ~0.35 MPa on a small area, i.e. densified foam), and one stiff enough backs the press but pre-loads SW1. Also tested: extra ledge ring round the SW1 pocket (SW1_POCKET_RING=1, no post): VHB tension 93-102 kPa, SW1 129/41/13 um at E 0.15/0.5/2: no.
Options: (a) rigid post with measured shim fit [rejected by owner rule]; (b) a printed flexure post (resin leaf spring, rate ~2 N/mm, self-centring over +-0.6 mm): same window problem unless preload <0.3 N, needs a CAD/FEM pass; (c) remove the tolerance, not the pad: bond the post to U1 top (cyanoacrylate-free: cured UV-resin / Kapton-VHB dot 0.1 on the post) with the stack hung from the lid, the tub closing onto it at assembly, i.e. a compliant adhesive layer 0.05-0.6 mm thick filling the gap (hand-buildable, gap-filling, but permanent bond to U1: service/rework cost); (d) accept a softer SW1 (taller travel by a longer-stroke switch, ECR for the part). Recommendation: (c) as a gap-filling 2-part silicone/UV potting dot, then a bench FEM check; owner decides. The rigid post (original ECR text) stays in the design until decided.
