## Change

Owner chose K4 end-to-end pod (spec O33) with board option B+ (docs/research/drastic/V9-k4-board.yaml: two stacked 4-layer boards ~11 x 12 mm, power board directly over the mic board; noise model PASS nominal, MARGINAL pessimistic, sim/noise/vertical_bplus.py). This ECR splits the schematic. Folds in ECR-0019 (J12/R18/D6 and net CC are not in the K4 netlists).

Source: hw/pod/k4/gen.py (copy of hw/pod/gen.py, original untouched; mz2 package set; `python3 hw/pod/k4/gen.py M` then `... P`, one board per process) -> hw/pod/k4/pod_k4_M.net / pod_k4_P.net, bom_jlc_M.csv / bom_jlc_P.csv. ERC (SKiDL, rev 2): 0 errors both boards.

### Partition (rev 2, 2026-10-08; rev 1 put U1 on M and L1 on P, which sent VLXSMPS and VDD11 through the connector: rejected in review)
Rule: no switching node crosses the B2B. VLXSMPS, L1 (within ~2 mm of the pin), C8/C9 (VDD11) and the C7 VDDSMPS input cap stay on U1's board. gen.py asserts that the set of crossing nets equals the BM28 pin-map nets.
- **M (lower board, 3 placed parts; mic port on its outer face):** U2 SPH0641LU4H-1 PDM mic, C13 100 nF on MIC_VDD, J20 BM28 receptacle. Nothing else.
- **P (upper board, 51 placed parts, J21 included):** everything else. U1 STM32U575 + C1-C12, L1, R1, R2 (33R clock damper at the MCU end), Y1; R15/R16; U3 charger + U4 LDO + their caps; dock protection; Q1/Q2 bridge + gate pulls, shunt, C14; SW1 + R10; LED feed; DNP pads J1-J5, J7-J10, TP1-TP10; J21 BM28 plug.
- Q1/Q2 stay with U1 on P: their gate nets (GA/GB) come straight from U1 and the 315 mA bridge loop (C14, LDO, Q1/Q2) never touches the connector. Both V9 aggressors (F01 bridge, F02 SMPS) are on the upper board over the mic, as the B+ noise model assumes.
- Cost of the rule: M is a mic carrier. The two-board split now buys only the plane-shielded vertical separation of the mic from the aggressors (V9 B+); it no longer shares the MCU load between boards. If the owner prefers, M can shrink to a mic daughter board smaller than 11 x 12 mm (open for the owner).

### B2B interface: Hirose BM28, 10 contacts (5 per row, 0.35 mm pitch)
P = plug BM28B0.6-10DP/2-0.35V(51) C424562 (Extended, 14198 in stock, $0.1992); M = receptacle BM28B0.6-10DS/2-0.35V(51) C424563 (Extended, 15959, $0.1894); JLC parts API 2026-10-08T02:09Z. Mated height 0.6 mm [T: search snippet, hirose.com 403, datasheet PDF not fetched]. Replaces the 30-pin C424570/C424571 of rev 1. 3 signals + 7 GND:
- pin 1 GND, 2 MIC_VDD, 3 MIC_CLK, 4 GND, 5 GND, 6 GND, 7 MIC_DATA, 8 GND, 9 GND, 10 GND (odd pins row A, even row B; pin n mates pin n [T]).
- MIC_CLK (3) has GND at 1 and 5 (same row) and 4 (facing); MIC_DATA (7) has GND at 5, 9 and 8; MIC_VDD (2) GND at 4 and 1. 3-4 MHz PDM edges thus have a return on every side.
- Currents: MIC_VDD ~1 mA (GPIO PA5 supply), PDM lines mA of capacitive drive. The 0.3 A [T] contact rating is irrelevant. VLXSMPS, VDD11, +3V0, I2C, USB, bridge, charger, button and LED nets no longer cross at all.
- Alternative (rev 1): L1 on M and U1 on M gave 28 pins but put the SMPS loop beside the mic; rejected for that reason. Owner decides.
- BM28 numbering: pin n mates pin n (plug/receptacle footprints mirrored) [T until the footprint is fetched, easyeda2kicad C424562/C424563 not yet run].

### Not done here
No placement, no routing, no footprint fetch, no noise re-run beyond sim/noise/vertical_bplus.py (re-run for rev 2: unchanged, 12.7 dB worst PSRR nominal, 4.8 dB pessimistic leak; V9 B_plus_noise.rerun_2026_10_08_partition_v2). Conduction through BM28 (A6 in V9) still unmodelled. Board length: the rev-1 30-contact BM28 was ~7 mm along the row (rev 2 10-contact: ~2.5 mm, [E]), so it runs along the 12 mm width; the connector costs ~2 mm of length per board [E].
