### B2B interface: Hirose BM28, 30 contacts (15 per row, 0.35 mm pitch), smallest that fits

Mated height 0.6 mm [T: search snippet]. 16 signal/power + 14 GND; the 20-contact C424566 leaves only 4 GND for the PDM trio and the D+/D- pair, so it does not fit.
