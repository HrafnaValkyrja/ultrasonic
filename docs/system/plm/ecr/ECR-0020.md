## Change

Owner chose K4 end-to-end pod (spec O33) with board option B+ (docs/research/drastic/V9-k4-board.yaml: two stacked 4-layer boards ~11 x 12 mm, power board directly over the mic board; noise model PASS nominal, MARGINAL pessimistic, sim/noise/vertical_bplus.py). This ECR splits the schematic. Folds in ECR-0019 (J12/R18/D6 and net CC are not in the K4 netlists).

Source: hw/pod/k4/gen.py (copy of hw/pod/gen.py, original untouched; mz2 package set; `python3 hw/pod/k4/gen.py M` then `... P`, one board per process) -> hw/pod/k4/pod_k4_M.net / pod_k4_P.net, bom_jlc_M.csv / bom_jlc_P.csv. ERC (SKiDL): 0 errors both boards.

### Partition (chosen to minimise B2B nets; one rule: a part goes with the pin it is wired to)
- **M (lower board, 22 placed parts; mic port on its outer face):** U1 STM32U575 (MCU) + C1-C7 (3V0 decoupling/bulk), C5/C6 VDDA, C8/C9 VDD11 caps, C10 NRST, R1 BOOT0, Y1 + C11/C12 (32.768 kHz crystal), U2 mic + C13 + R2, R15/R16 I2C pull-ups, C19 VBAT_SENSE hold cap, J20 (BM28 receptacle); DNP pads TP1-TP5 (SWD, NRST, 3V0, GND), TP7-TP10 (printf, MDF dots).
- **P (upper board, 32 placed parts):** U3 BQ25180 charger + C15/C16/C21, U4 LDO + C17/C18/R20, D4/D5/U6 dock protection, R12/R13 VBUS sense, R8/R9 VBAT divider, RT1 NTC, **L1** (MCU core SMPS inductor), **Q1/Q2** bridge + R3-R6 gate pulls + R21/R22/C22/R23 shunt and I_SENSE filter + C14 22 uF, SW1 + R10, R14 LED feed, J21 (BM28 plug); DNP pads J1-J5, J7-J10, TP6 VSYS.
- Why bridge + L1 on P: the B+ noise model puts both aggressors (F01 bridge, F02 SMPS) on the upper board over the mic. The 315 mA bridge pulses then stay on P (C14, LDO, Q1/Q2 never touch the connector); only the MCU's own load crosses +3V0.

### B2B interface: Hirose BM28, 30 contacts (15 per row, 0.35 mm pitch)
P = plug BM28B0.6-30DP/2-0.35V(51) C424570 (Extended, 20072 in stock, $0.1829); M = receptacle BM28B0.6-30DS/2-0.35V(51) C424571 (Extended, 737, $0.2096); JLC parts API 2026-10-08. Mated height 0.6 mm [T: search snippet, hirose.com 403, datasheet PDF fetch timed out]. 19 signal/power pins + 11 GND:
- +3V0 x2 (pins 2, 28), VLXSMPS (3, guarded by GND 1/4/5), VDD11 (6), GA_P/GA_N/GB_P/GB_N (7-10), I_SENSE (12), USB_DP/USB_DM (13/15, GND 14/16 between), I2C_SCL/SDA (18/19), CHG_INT (20), TS (22), VBAT_SENSE (23), VBUS_SENSE (24), LED_K (26), BTN (27); GND x11 (1, 4, 5, 11, 14, 16, 17, 21, 25, 29, 30) = a ground within 2 pins of every net.
- Pin sizing: Hirose BM28 rating 0.3 A per signal contact (power-variant contacts rated higher, 5 A, in the Hirose press text) [T: secondary source, not datasheet-verified]. Worst current on any one contact: +3V0 to M 2 x 0.3 A for an MCU load < 0.1 A mean (USB active ~0.15 A) = OK; VLXSMPS/VDD11 carry the SMPS inductor current (core 1.1 V load tens of mA, ripple 108 mA pp, switch-current limit not read) on ONE contact each: margin unproven [T], the main electrical risk. The 315 mA bridge peak never crosses.
- Alternative counts: L1 on M (no VLX/VDD11 crossing): 28 pins, but the SMPS loop returns beside the mic (B+ premise lost). Bridge on M: -5 pins (gates, I_SENSE) but 315 mA over 2 contacts. Owner decides.
- BM28 numbering: pin n mates pin n (plug/receptacle footprints mirrored) [T until the footprint is fetched, easyeda2kicad C424570/C424571 not yet run].

### Not done here
No placement, no routing, no footprint fetch, no noise re-run. Conduction through BM28 (A6 in V9) still unmodelled. Board length: a 30-contact BM28 is ~7 mm along the row, so it runs along the 12 mm width; the connector costs ~2 mm of length per board [E].
