# Datasheet audit findings: PB5 floating (UCPD dead-battery on PA15), charger register plan, thermal 0.3 W, Off-mode back-feed

Status: proposed
Raised: 2026-10-02 by claude
Items changed: SUB-POWER, SUB-DOCK-USB, SUB-PROCESSING, SUB-DEBUG-TEST, REG-BOARD

## Change

Source: docs/system/audit-datasheet-claims.md (2026-10-02; BQ25180 SLUSE99C Rev C, AN2606 Rev 69, DS13737 Rev 10, RM0456 Rev 7, ES0499 Rev 12, AN5373 Rev 7; page + quote per claim). Doc number fixes already applied.
SCHEMATIC (batch into the next schematic revision after the simplification study; do not change gen.py mid-study):
 S1 PB5 is NC (floating). DS13737: the UCPD dead-battery pull-down on PA15 (UCPD1_CC1) is activated by HIGH on PB5 (UCPD1_DBCC1). PA15 = CHG_INT (open-drain /INT, R17 10k to 3V0): a 5.1k pull-down reads ~1.0 V = stuck-low interrupt. Fix: tie PB5 to GND (ST's own advice); firmware also sets PWR_UCPDR.UCPD_DBDIS early.
 S2 PB15 pull-down is activated by HIGH on PB14 (= I2C_SDA, pulled up). Matters for ECR-0003 (GB_N on PB15): a 5.1k pull-down on the N gate holds it OFF (harmless), but it stays until UCPD_DBDIS is set: confirm in firmware order; N gate cannot be driven high before that.
 S3 C21/C17 (VSYS): TI recommends 25 V caps on IN and SYS; the pod uses 10 V parts (C19702 CL10A106KP8NNNC). Derated value still > 1 µF minimum: keep, or move to 16/25 V if the part exists as Basic.
FIRMWARE / REGISTER PLAN (feeds docs/sim/firmware-emulation.yaml and sub-power):
 F1 ICHG powers up at 10 mA; firmware writes 170 mA (code 44) only after enumeration / known cable; ILIM 100 mA until enumeration; VINDPM = 4.2 V (default Disabled).
 F2 Ship mode fires when TS/MR is RELEASED after the hold, not at 10 s; EN_PUSH=0 only covers battery mode, so set PB_LPRESS_ACTION=00 to disarm it docked and on battery. J8-J9 bridge / PA2 driven low still threatens it.
 F3 Watchdog default: 160 s register revert (no power cycle), armed only after the first I2C access; option B power-cycles SYS and also trips in Off: disable in the boot stub before any long DFU.
 F4 ROM DFU: PA2 = USART2_TX push-pull pull-up drives TS high ("cold"): charging pauses during DFU. Bridge pins are safe in DFU (AN2606 Table 199).
 F5 Off-mode current: PB4 (NJTRST pull-up at reset) and PB3 back-feed the mic from PA5-gated supply: ~60 µA vs the 16-24 µA Off budget. Firmware sets PB3/PB4 to analog/no-pull via PWR_PUCR/PDCR before Off; in reset/ROM DFU it cannot: accept or add a series element.
SAFETY (R23, ECR-0009): plan U3 at ~0.3 W (+31 C at 107 C/W JEDEC) at start of CC, not 0.2 W; battery-tracking SYS_REG is not a lever; taper ICHG at low VBAT; thermal placement of U3 vs the pouch (cross-domain risk 13) stands. Owner reviews against SLUSE99C.
