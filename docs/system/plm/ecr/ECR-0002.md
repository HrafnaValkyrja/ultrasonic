Status: implemented (awaiting owner review)

## Change

Routing fixes from the congestion diagnosis (docs/diagrams/routing-congestion.png, agent report 2026-10-01),
all checked courtyard-free in memory:
1. I2C pull-ups beside U3 on B: R16 (16.3, 4.75, 270), R15 (17.5, 4.75, 270), R17 (18.7, 4.75, 270); C21 to (21.0, 2.0, 0).
   Removes the R16 airline and the 22 mm of In2 detours to R15/R17 at the bottom-left.
2. Swap C15 (VBUS cap) and C16 (VBAT cap) so each sits beside its own U3 ball (A2 VBUS at x 17.6, C2 VBAT at x 16.8);
   VSYS (B2) then exits straight up to a via.
3. C7 (VDDSMPS input) to (13.8, 11.4, 0, B) once 1 is done: frees the east escape lane, shortens the SMPS loop.
4. R11 next to MCU pin 31 if ECR-0003 is not taken.
ON HOLD until the simplification study: parts in this cluster may change.
