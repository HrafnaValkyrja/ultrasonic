## Where the schematic symbols and footprints came from
None of them was drawn by hand from a datasheet:
- **STM32U575, SPH0641, MCP73831:** KiCad 10's standard library.
- **LCSC parts:** converted from EasyEDA by easyeda2kicad.
- **PMCXB290UE:** its symbol was written in `hw/pod/gen.py` from datasheet Table 2. Its footprint is EasyEDA's, and it's already known to be smaller than Nexperia's land pattern.

Pin-by-pin and pad-by-pad checks against the datasheets are part of the adversarial audit (2026-09-30).

## ST documents: Rev 8 → current, what changed for this design (checked 2026-09-30)
**Method.** Tables were extracted from both PDFs with PyMuPDF and compared row by row for the pins we use. Sections were text-diffed. Revision-history entries were read in full.
- **Pinout and alternate functions:** unchanged for all 16 pins and every power pin we use. The UFQFPN48-SMPS column is identical in Rev 8 and Rev 10 (e.g. PB3 = 39, PH3-BOOT0 = 44, VLXSMPS = 20, VDD11 = 23/46, VDD = 25/36/48), and matches `hw/pod/pod.net`. ADF1_CCK0/SDI0 on PB3/PB4 (AF3) and TIM1_CH1/CH1N/CH2/CH2N on PA8/PA7/PA9/PB0 are unchanged. The only AF differences anywhere are USART "RTS_DE" → "RTS/DE" renames.
- **Package pins:** this package has no VDDUSB, VREF± or VDDIO2 pins (Rev 10 Table 26), which closes A3 open item 6.
- **§3.9.1 power supply schemes:** wording only (introduces VSW, the supply for PC13–15 in VBAT mode). No effect; VBAT is tied to VDD.
- **Stop 2 on SMPS (Table 58):** only the hot-temperature values rose, by 3–4% (e.g. 135 → 140). Room-temperature values are unchanged, so spec D12's ~4–9 µA stands.
- **ES0499, new since Rev 10 and relevant:**
  - **"Spurious MSI PLL unlock" (Rev 12).** MSI in PLL mode, which is our clock tree (D14), can falsely report a failure and unlock. Accuracy then degrades for 0.6–1 ms until re-locked. It's more likely when cold and at low VCORE. **Firmware must handle the unlock interrupt by disabling and re-enabling MSI PLL mode.** No hardware change: the mic clock and PWM share the source, so they stay coherent.
  - **"Unexpected PWM output when using ocref_clr" (Rev 11), no workaround.** It affects combined and asymmetric PWM modes with ocref_clr. We don't use these. **Rule: any future bridge fault cut-off uses the TIM1 break function (BKIN pin or an internal break source such as mdf1_break0, RM0456 Table 541), never ocref_clr.** (clarified 2026-10-07, sub-processing issue 6)
- **ES0499, existing errata the plan hadn't recorded:** §2.2.5, a hang-up if a wake-up request arrives just before Stop 2 entry while PLL2/PLL3/HSI48/SHSI are on. **Firmware: switch those off and wait for their RDY flags to clear before Stop 2.** Already covered: §2.2.8 (MSIK keeps running as the ADF kernel clock) is in A3. LSE low and medium-low drive are unusable (§2.2.3, §2.2.16), and our crystal runs at high drive.
- **RM0456 Rev 7:** updated the PWR, RCC, GPIO, GPDMA, ADC4 and TIM1/TIM8 chapters (plus others we don't use) without detail. This is register-level behaviour, so **diff these chapters against Rev 6 before writing firmware**. The board doesn't depend on it.
- **AN5373 Rev 7 vs the schematic:**
  - These match: 2.2 µH coil VLXSMPS→VDD11; two 2.2 µF on VDD11 (layout: return them to the VSSSMPS pin); 100 nF per VDD pin; VDDA 100 nF + 1 µF.
  - **One shortfall:** AN5373 asks for "10 µF typical, 4.7 µF minimum" bulk on VDD for the package. C4 is 4.7 µF nominal in 0402 X5R, so below the minimum under DC bias. C7 (10 µF, same +3V0 net, at the SMPS input) and C14 (22 µF) are on the net, but not necessarily close to the VDD pins. **Fix: make C4 10 µF 0603 (Basic C19702), or place C7 so it serves the VDD pins.**
