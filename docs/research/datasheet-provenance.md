## Where the schematic symbols and footprints came from
None of them was drawn by hand from a datasheet:
- **STM32U575, SPH0641, MCP73831:** KiCad 10's standard library.
- **LCSC parts:** converted from EasyEDA by easyeda2kicad.
- **PMCXB290UE:** its symbol was written in `hw/pod/gen.py` from datasheet Table 2. Its footprint is EasyEDA's, and it's already known to be smaller than Nexperia's land pattern.

Pin-by-pin and pad-by-pad checks against the datasheets are part of the adversarial audit (2026-09-30).
