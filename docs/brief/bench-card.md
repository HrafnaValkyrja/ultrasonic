# Bench card (hands-on chores, no ordered parts)

![bench card](../diagrams/bench-card.png)

1. Body measure (queue A-TODAY-BODY-MEASURE): 20 min, helper. E10 pen-tip vision edge x3, calipers on the arm 50 mm behind hinge (CAD assumes 2.5 x 5.0 mm), E9 ruler + mouth-open photos, optional E12 100 g hang (spec E9/E10/E12).
2. Mass wear test: 2 h. Queue A-TODAY-MASS-TARGET is closed (superseded by sim/checks/pod_mass.py, K4 9.05-10.15 g worn, backlog PHYS-8), so this is an optional comfort check at ~10 g per side, 50 mm behind the hinge.
3. Shokz listening (queue A-SHOKZ-BENCH, memory shokz-salvage-bench): tragus listening with sim/out/nature/{1_audible_only,2_translated_only,3_combined}.wav, ~1 N; teardown dimensions of their cell/board for packaging. Qualitative only; never reuse their cell.

Other hardware items in backlog.yaml (K4-MAG-HEAD, K4-BM28-BENCH, K4-DOCK-CYCLES, K4-L1-BUZZ, K4-WINDOW-SEAL) need ordered samples or a built stack, so they are not on the card. A-PHYSICAL-FIRSTWINS (E1 coil R/L, E2) waits on the exciter (O21).
